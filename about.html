About Me

I investigate how the brain and biological systems transform molecular signals into behavior, with a strong focus on translating these insights into drug discovery. My work centers on converting complex neural and biological data into predictive, data-driven frameworks with therapeutic potential.

Currently, I operate at the intersection of systems neuroscience, molecular biology, drug target identification, and machine learning–driven predictive modeling. I focus on decoding complex neural states from high-dimensional data, driven by an unwavering commitment to advancing mental health research. I approach the brain as an adaptive yet inherently vulnerable system, studied through next-generation sequencing, functional and molecular imaging, spatial transcriptomics, integrative multi-omics, and probabilistic modeling.

I develop reproducible pipelines and scalable analytical frameworks that translate biological complexity into actionable insights, particularly within precision medicine for neuropsychiatric and neurodegenerative disorders. I am comfortable navigating stochastic systems, noisy datasets, and abstract models, with a focus on extracting meaningful signal from high-dimensional variability.

I bring a structured and analytical mindset to neuroscience and molecular biology, grounded in computational rigor. I approach data analysis, modeling, and coding with intention—building solutions that are not only technically robust but also impactful and accessible. My work is guided by a user- and patient-centered perspective, rooted in the belief that without real-world relevance, even the most rigorous research lacks purpose.

I also operate with a zen-inspired mindset, valuing clarity, minimalism, and depth in both scientific inquiry and data analysis. Beyond research, I am drawn to open landscapes and mountains—spaces that recalibrate perspective and reinforce the balance between precision and intuition. I document these explorations through a world photography map, capturing structure, pattern, and environment across geographies.
